CANDLE STRUCTURE

Every candle, on every time frame, is described by the same four prices.

OPEN

The price at the start of the candle's time period.

HIGH

The highest price reached during the period.

LOW

The lowest price reached during the period.

CLOSE

The price at the end of the period.

BODY

The thick part between the open and the close.

WICK

The thin lines above and below the body, showing the high and the low.

HOW A BIG CANDLE IS BUILT

Take the open of the first small candle, the close of the last small candle, the highest high and the lowest low from all of them. Put those four prices together and you have the bigger candle.

This is why traders check higher time frames first: they show the overall direction, and the smaller time frames then show the detail for the entry.
